# What PR #268 could break

**# New Article page fixes** · [https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268](https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268)

> PR 268 reworks the New Article editor page; the Publish Article flow and all editor fields need a full retest, and the edit path was never seen in the app.

## Re-test first

1. Create and publish a new article from the editor page. Check the Publish Article button on the editor page sends the article. *(High/Medium confidence)*
2. Check every field on the editor page: Article Title, description, body (markdown) and Enter tags. Adding a tag is part of the change. *(High confidence)*
3. Editing an existing article was never seen in the running app. Test it by hand with an article you own. Check Publish Article saves the changes. *(Low/Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
4. On the article page, as a signed-in user, post a comment with the Write a comment field and the Post Comment button. *(Medium confidence)*
5. Check the Your Feed link on the home page and on tag pages when signed in. Confirm the home feed list still loads. *(Medium confidence)*
6. Quick check of Favorite Article and Follow on the article page. *(Medium confidence)*

## Editor page (highest confidence)

- The editor page at /editor is affected. Five elements on it changed. *(High/Medium confidence)*
- The title, description, body and tags fields each lose their only proof of coverage. Every element that proves them is changed by this PR. *(High confidence)*
- The Publish Article button on the editor page also loses its coverage for the same reason. *(High confidence)*
- The Publish Article flow on the editor page passes through the changed screen. Its last step creates the article. *(High confidence)*

## Signed-in changes on other pages (medium confidence)

- The sign-in check changed. It decides what shows on the article page and the home page. *(Medium confidence)*
- Comment posting loses its coverage. Every element that proves it is changed. *(Medium confidence)*
- The Your Feed toggle also loses its coverage. The home feed list is affected too. *(Medium confidence)*
- Favorite Article and Follow on the article page pass through changed UI. *(Medium confidence)*
- Reading article data and the current user are called by the changed code. Both were seen in the app. *(Medium confidence)*

## Never seen in the running app

- Saving changes to an existing article was never seen in the app. No crawled button triggered it. It needs an article-owner state. *(Low/Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
- The edit page at /editor/:slug was never seen in the app. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
- Two Publish Article flows on the edit page come from code only. One creates and one updates. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*

## Pages affected, lower confidence

- The article page, the home page, paginated feeds and tag pages are affected. Check they still load. *(Medium confidence)*
- Signed-out view: the home banner text and the Sign in and Sign up links on the article page are affected. Confidence is low. *(Low/Medium confidence)*
- Some text was only seen in code, never in the app. This covers the Realworld link, the demo text on the home page, and the line about adding comments. *(Low confidence, not seen in the app)*
