# What PR #268 could break

**# New Article page fixes** · [https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268](https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268)

> The New Article editor page changed. Publishing a new article is the main flow to retest. Comments and the Your Feed tab may also be affected.

## Re-test first

1. Publish a new article from the editor page. Fill in the title, description, body and tags, then press Publish Article. This flow was seen in the running app. *(High confidence)*
2. Check that every editor field is still there and works: title, description, body in markdown, and Enter tags. *(High confidence)*
3. Edit and republish an existing article. This was never seen in the running app, so test it by hand with an article you own. *(Low/Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
4. Post a comment on an article while signed in. Medium confidence. *(Medium confidence)*
5. Check the Your Feed tab on the home page and on tag pages. Medium confidence. *(Medium confidence)*

## Editor page (high confidence, seen in the app)

- The editor page at the new-article and edit-article addresses is affected. Five elements on it changed. *(High/Medium confidence)*
- The Article Title field changed. *(High confidence)*
- The description field changed. Its placeholder asks what the article is about. *(High confidence)*
- The markdown body field changed. *(High confidence)*
- The Enter tags field changed. *(High confidence)*
- The Publish Article button changed. A test run of its action matched the create-article call. *(High confidence)*

## Article page and home feed (medium confidence, seen in the app)

- The comment box and the Post Comment button on the article page are affected. They show only to signed-in users. *(Medium confidence)*
- The Your Feed tab is affected on the home page and on tag pages. *(Medium confidence)*
- The home page article list is affected. It draws from Your Feed, Global Feed or a selected tag. *(Medium confidence)*
- The article page loads an article and the signed-in user's data. Both were seen in the app. *(Medium confidence)*

## Pages touched (lower confidence)

- Article detail page and its markdown rendering. *(Medium confidence)*
- Home page, Your Feed view and paging. *(Medium confidence)*
- Tag pages and their paging. *(Medium confidence)*
- Signed-out view: the Angular frontend banner on the home and tag pages, plus the sign up and Sign in links on the article page. Low confidence. *(Medium confidence)*

## Never seen in the running app

- Saving changes to an existing article was never seen in the app. No crawled button triggered it. It needs a signed-in owner of an article to check. *(Low/Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
- Some home page text was seen only in code: the Realworld link and the demo description. *(Low confidence, not seen in the app)*
- The text about adding comments on an article was seen only in code. *(Low confidence, not seen in the app)*
- The signed-out heading on the home and tag pages has low confidence. *(Low confidence)*

## Coverage that is lost

- For each editor field and for the Publish Article button, everything that proves it is changed by this PR. Do not trust the old checks. Retest them by hand. *(High confidence)*
- The same applies to the comment box, the Post Comment button, the comment call and the Your Feed tab. *(Medium confidence)*
- The save-changes call for existing articles has no UI check to re-run it. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
