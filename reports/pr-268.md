## What the change does

- The editor now loads an article and checks the author only when the address has a slug. Before, opening the New Article page asked for an article called 'undefined' and ran the author check. That broke the page. *(High confidence)*
- The show-when-signed-in wrapper now builds its content only once. Repeated sign-in signals no longer duplicate it. It is never reset after the content is cleared. After a logout and a new login, the content may not show again. *(Medium confidence)*

## Coverage at risk

- For the title, description and body fields, every test that checks them goes through changed UI. The same holds for the 'Enter tags' field and the Publish Article button. Until re-run and passing, they have no trusted coverage. *(High confidence)*

## Never seen in the running app

- Three texts were found only in code and never seen in the app: 'Realworld', the demo description text on the home page, and 'or to add comments on this article.' on the article page. *(Low confidence, not seen in the app)*
