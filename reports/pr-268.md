# What PR #268 could break

**# New Article page fixes** · [https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268](https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268)

> New Article page fixes: the editor page and its Publish Article flow are the main risk.

## Re-test first

1. Open the editor page at /editor as a signed-in user. Check the Article Title, description, body and Enter tags fields all show and accept input. *(High confidence)*
2. Run the full flow to publish a new article with the Publish Article button on the editor page. *(High confidence)*
3. Edit and republish an existing article. This was never seen in the running app, so test it by hand. *(Low/Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
4. Post a comment on an article as a signed-in user. Also check the Your Feed toggle on the home page. *(Medium confidence)*

## Editor page (high confidence, seen in the app)

- The editor page for creating and editing articles is affected. Five elements on it changed. *(High confidence)*
- The Article Title field changed. *(High confidence)*
- The "What's this article about?" field changed. *(High confidence)*
- The "Write your article (in markdown)" field changed. *(High confidence)*
- The Enter tags field changed. Adding tags is part of the change. *(High confidence)*
- The Publish Article button changed. Submitting should create the article. *(High confidence)*
- The flow to publish a new article passes through the changed screen. It ends in the create-article call. *(High confidence)*

## Article page comments and home feed (medium confidence, seen in the app)

- The Write a comment field and Post Comment button on the article page may be affected. The effect comes from a change to the signed-in-only display logic. *(Medium confidence)*
- The Post a comment flow passes through this changed UI. *(Medium confidence)*
- The Your Feed toggle on the home page and tag pages may be affected. So may the article list on the home page. *(Medium confidence)*
- The app also reads a single article and the current user. These links are weaker. *(Medium confidence)*

## Never seen in the running app

- Updating an existing article was never seen in the running app. It needs an article-owner state, and no crawled button triggered it. *(Low/Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
- Some texts were seen only in code: Realworld, the demo description, and "or to add comments on this article." Confidence is low. *(Low confidence, not seen in the app)*

## Coverage that cannot vouch for this change

- For these checks, every element that proves them was changed by this PR. They cannot confirm the page still works. Check them by hand: the four editor fields, the Publish Article button, Write a comment, Post Comment and Your Feed. *(High/Medium confidence)*
- The update-article call has no UI coverage to re-check it. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*

## Pages touched (lower confidence)

- These pages are touched only indirectly: home, home feed variants, tag pages and the article page. A quick look is enough. *(Medium confidence)*
- Sign-in and sign-up links, and the Angular frontend text for signed-out visitors, are weakly linked. *(Medium confidence)*
