# What PR #268 could break

**# New Article page fixes** · [https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268](https://github.com/realworld-apps/angular-realworld-example-app/pull/268)

> The New Article page no longer asks for an article that does not exist. It only loads the article and current user when the address has an article slug. The change also stops gated views from stacking up on repeated sign-in events. But they may not come back after logout and a second login.

## Re-test first

1. Open the New article page at /editor and the Edit article page at /editor/article-slug-here. Check that both load. The new page should open without errors or redirects. *(High/Medium confidence)*
2. On the Edit article page, change an article and save it. Check that the update goes through. *(High confidence)*
3. On both editor pages, check the tags, body, description and title fields. They should show up and accept input. On the edit page they should hold the existing article's values. *(High confidence)*
4. Check the Publish Article button on the editor page, on both the new and edit pages. *(High confidence)*
5. Signed in, open an article. Check that the comment box and the Post Comment button appear. Post a comment. *(Medium confidence)*
6. Signed in, check that the Your Feed tab shows on the home page and on tag pages. *(Medium confidence)*
7. Run the full flow: publish a new article. *(High confidence)*
8. Run the full flow: edit your own published article. *(High confidence)*
9. Run the flow: delete your own article. It creates the article through the editor first. *(Medium confidence)*

## Logout and second login risk

- Gated views are now created only once and the guard is never reset after logout. After logout and a second login, or the reverse, a gated view may not come back. *(Medium confidence)*
- Try this in one session without reloading the page. Log in, log out, then log in again. Check the Your Feed tab and the comment box. *(Medium confidence)*
- Also log out and check that the Sign in and sign up links come back on the article page. *(Low confidence)*

## Checks that rest only on changed items

- For these checks, every item that proves them is changed by this PR. Existing coverage cannot be trusted here. They are the editor fields, the Publish Article button, the edit save call, the comment box and button, the comment post call and the Your Feed tab. *(High/Medium confidence)*

## Never seen in the running app

- These items were only seen in code. They were never seen in the running app. They are the Realworld link and the demo text on the home page, and the 'or to add comments on this article.' text on the article page. Confirm by eye whether they show for signed-out users. *(Low confidence, not seen in the app)*
