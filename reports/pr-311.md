> PR 311: article edits in the editor now use PUT. The edit path was never seen in the running app.

## Re-test first

1. Publish a new article from the editor page. Click the Publish Article button and confirm the article is created. This flow was seen in the running app. *(High confidence)*
2. Edit an existing article and republish it. Check that the update saves. This flow was never seen in the running app, so test it by hand. It needs an article owner login. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
3. Open the editor page at /editor and at an existing article's editor address. Confirm it loads for both creating and editing. *(High confidence)*

## What changed

- The editor now sends article updates with a PUT request instead of the previous method. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
- The Publish Article button on the editor page is the changed control. It was seen in the running app when logged in. *(High confidence)*

## Affected pages and controls

- The editor page for creating and editing articles is affected. It was seen in the running app. *(High confidence)*
- The Publish Article button requirement is affected. Every element that proves it is changed by this PR, so existing coverage of it is lost. *(High confidence)*
- The /editor route showing New article is also touched, with lower confidence. It was seen in the running app. *(Medium confidence)*

## Affected flows

- Publish a new article passes through the changed button and ends in the create call. It was seen in the running app. *(High confidence)*
- Edit and republish an existing article is affected. It was never seen in the running app. Confidence is moderate. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*

## Not seen in the running app

- The PUT call for article updates exists in code. No crawled button triggered it. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
- The requirement that the app calls PUT for updates was never shown in the crawled states (anon, authed). It needs the owner state. There is no UI coverage to re-check it. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
- The edit and republish flow is seen only in code. *(Medium confidence, not seen in the app)*
